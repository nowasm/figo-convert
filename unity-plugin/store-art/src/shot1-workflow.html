How it works

One click from design to prefab

1

Pick a design

A Figma .fig (File → Save local copy…), a figo canvas.json, or Figma REST JSON — no plugins, no API keys.

2

Convert

Tools → Figo Prefab Importer… or right-click the asset → Figo → Convert to UGUI Prefabs.

3

Drop into your scene

UGUI prefabs with baked sprites and .meta files, imported straight into Assets/.

$ figo2unity starfall_menu.canvas.json Assets/FigoImported
loading starfall_menu.canvas.json
  menu     → menu.prefab     (186 objects)
  settings → settings.prefab (219 objects)
RESULT: OK, 2 prefab(s), 33 unique sprite(s)
Sample included: “Starfall Arena” menu + settings — converts in seconds
